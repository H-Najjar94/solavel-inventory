# Reviewed stock base-unit conversion

The owner endpoint `/api/v1/items/{item}/base-unit-conversion/preview` is read-only. `apply` requires both item-management and settings permissions plus organization-wide warehouse authority. A rational quantity ratio is explicit: **new quantity = old quantity × numerator ÷ denominator** (12 pieces to 1 box uses 1/12). It is never inferred from an invoice or a selected label.

The initial historical kernel supports positive, untracked opening stock under average costing or FIFO with entirely unconsumed native opening layers. It covers every warehouse and zero-balance cached costs. It rejects negative/reserved stock, serial/lot/bin/variant coordinates, consumed or previously corrected FIFO layers, open or other document references, dimensional packaging metadata, existing item-specific conversions/supplier terms, precision loss and incompatible source history. The preview identifies each blocker. A label change is never substituted for conversion.

The item, balance/layer/replenishment projections and request are locked in one transaction. Existing document and ledger rows remain immutable. Paired, explicitly labeled old-unit OUT/new-unit IN rows preserve each warehouse's value. Current FIFO layer identities, source ledger references and received dates remain; exact quantities and costs change together. Catalog prices/weight and replenishment quantities are rescaled exactly. Historical reports label each epoch, and as-of valuation selects the applicable unit. Reclassification is excluded from physical sales/movement activity metrics. Old opening reversals use their original value and normalized current quantity through the normal opening-stock action. Historical FIFO correction plans crossing a unit boundary are blocked; stale balance repair plans are rechecked under the item lock.

`item_base_unit_conversions` stores the immutable before/after snapshots, exact ratio, evidence, actor, effective date, prior ledger cutover, boundary rows, reviewed revision and idempotency UUID. A supporting item attachment is required by the UI/API and its SHA256 is verified server-side; a referenced attachment cannot be deleted. The migration is additive and forward-only. Replay verifies canonical columns, indexes, FKs and immutable triggers. New ordinary movements before the latest effective date are rejected; same-day movement is supported. Future-dated conversion is not supported.

Connected organizations additionally require one verified active organization mapping, verified item/old/new-unit mappings, settled catalog synchronization, an authorized actor and an open mapped SolaCount accounting period. The existing signed owner command `catalog.unit_transition` carries a durable event, not a financial stock movement. Stock becomes `finance_pending`; affected writes and aggregate quantity views are held until Finance acknowledges the exact event/hash/revision and verified target identities. Finance records the mapped catalog epoch only: no second Stock writer, payable or journal entry. Transport leases and the stable event UUID make timeout/retry recovery idempotent. Failure remains visible and retryable; it does not roll back a remotely accepted epoch or create another conversion.

During application cutover `/etc/solavel/maintenance/unit-conversions-paused` blocks new applies but permits preview and recovery. The committed capability marker identifies epoch-compatible rollback targets. After real events exist, reverting to a pre-engine application is unsafe and must be refused by release tooling. Production business records are never test fixtures.
